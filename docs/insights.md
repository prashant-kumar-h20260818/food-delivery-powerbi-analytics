# Key Business Insights

The repository uses a reproducible synthetic dataset created specifically for portfolio use.

- **₹164.91M revenue** generated across **200,000 orders** with an **AOV of ₹824.57**.
- **20,000 customers** generated an average of **10 orders/customer**; **50.28%** of customers are repeat customers.
- **56,000 restaurant registries** span **8 cities** and **12 cuisine categories**.
- **96,240 orders (48.12%)** are flagged late, while average delivery time is **44.55 minutes**.
- **20,000 orders (10.00%)** are cancelled.
- **59.10%** of order volume is in the Budget cost segment.
- **Bengaluru** is the highest-revenue city in this generated dataset.
- **North Indian** is the most frequently ordered cuisine.
- **Kolkata** has the highest late-delivery rate and is the strongest operational-improvement candidate.

> These observations describe the generated portfolio dataset, not real Zomato/Swiggy performance.
